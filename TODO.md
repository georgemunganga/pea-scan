# QR landing page scope

## [x] 1. Fast mobile scan experience
- A visitor opening the QR link immediately sees Pure Essence Apothecary’s name, concise beauty/skincare description, and primary actions.
- The page is a compact contact card, not a long company website or a story-heavy page; keep it easy to scan and use on a phone.
- Make the shop, WhatsApp, call, and email actions easy to tap without reading through long sections.

## [x] 2. Essential business details
- Show both verified shop locations and link each to Google Maps directions.
- Show the published opening hours in one compact line.
- Keep only the short product overview needed to orient visitors; link to the existing shop for details.

## [x] 3. Contact-card utilities
- Let visitors save the business contact as a vCard.
- Let visitors share the profile and access/download a QR code for its page.
- Link only to the verified Instagram and Facebook profiles.
- Use concise labels, accessible tap targets, and a mobile layout that avoids unnecessary scrolling.

## [x] 4. Product and brand-promotion showcase
- Mix the official Baobab Nourishing Serum photo and linked featured-product cards with concise brand-promotion slides.
- Use a dark cacao-to-transparent backdrop with subtle smooth animation and smooth slide changes; provide manual navigation and pause/play controls, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Position the showcase beside the contact card on wider screens and below it on phones so essential contact actions remain first.
